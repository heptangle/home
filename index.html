Who am I?

I’m Borna. I study how world-building emerges through word-building discourse, the underlying patterns of contemporary political contentions and conflicts, and foresight through scenario building and forecast modelling.

At the macro-level, the philosophies of Prof. Braidotti and Prof. Barad on critical posthumanism and planetary futures form the object of my work as a researcher, and at the downstream, I apply their frame of knowledge acquisition to study the causal mechanisms of the post-anthropocene futures. This includes studying discourse in social mobilisation, the language through which politics is constructed and understood, the intra-active networks of agencies that shape political relations.

Having studied and lived in seven countries in 20 years; I've come to experience first hand the interconnectivity of the issues and resolutions in our increasingly globalised world; and by choosing to dedicate my telos to the acquisition and sharing of knowledge in Academia, I've taken it as my part in the story of our species to be a small contributor to our anthropic solutions.

What is Heptangle?

Heptangle is a heuristics system for producing anticipatory knowledge and providence on political futures through an applied combination of Foresight and Forecast. Foresight prefiguration inserts partial observers and axioms into a field so that calculation can run ahead of appearance, while forecast empiricism equips inquiry with atlases and rule-sets that standardise measurement and coerce phenomena into calculable form. Providential knowledge arises from architecturing a preferred outcome within the field of potential futures and its five internal gradations:

  • Potentiality encompasses everything beyond the present time
  • Possibilities are future knowledge of "might happens", a set of states that can be conceived but are not yet grounded in evidence.
  • Plausiblities are current knowledge of "could happen", derived from causal reasoning and consistent with known mechanisms.
  • Probable is the current trends of "likely to happen", where;
  • Projections are the "most probable" to happen.
The preferred outcome occupies the liminal zone between the plausible, the probable, and the projected. Providence, in this framework, is the delineation of the pathway leading from the field of potentialities toward that preferable configuration. It is operationalised through the heptad method described below, which transforms open potential into structured vidence and, ultimately, directed action.

Atlas of Providence

Horizon Construction

The field of inquiry is deliberately constructed rather than discovered. One must establish the observer position, time horizon, and foundational axioms that make the domain intelligible. Rather than simply defining what to study, this stage designs the lens and metric space through which events can emerge. It functions as the deliberate installation of a measurement framework.

Frame Calibration

This component standardises the analytical field through shared metrics, indices, spatial grids, and temporal baselines. Frame calibration formalises how a phenomenon become measurable.

Axiomatic Projection

Ideal observers and counterfactuals are defined to drive modelled inference. This approach replaces traditional factor identification with the creation of theoretical operators—structural equations, agent classes, or imagined entities—that allow the system to advance beyond currently available evidence.

Agential Realism

This stage examines how far each factor can reorganise the model's internal geometry. Variables are treated as existing in superposition—holding multiple potential states simultaneously until observation or intervention collapses them into specific configurations. Techniques such as cross-impact balances and sensitivity analysis are applied to map entangled agencies, where the measurement or activation of one factor instantaneously affects the state space of distant or seemingly unrelated variables. Informed intuition guides the detection of unmodelled couplings and latent mediators that exist in quantum-like indeterminacy before political events force their actualisation.

Scenario Composition

Multiple projected states are composed into possible worlds that include breakpoints, reversals, and emergent causal structures. The morphological grid becomes a design space for structural innovation rather than merely sampling consistent outcomes.

Apparition Modelling

Each composed scenario is tested for disruptive or untimely events—wild cards, regime shifts, or emergent agencies—that disrupt sequential causation with simultaneous disruptions. This phase transforms the search for discontinuities into a formal modelling task that evaluates how systems behave under conditions of surprise.

Autopoiesis

Iteration is inverse induction. The outcome is an operational model that reshapes decision systems—dashboards, policy simulators, or narrative prototypes—through which new data are continuously absorbed and the horizon recalibrated. Foresight becomes a self-updating methodology that integrates empirical forecast with creative scenario design within a single feedback architecture.
